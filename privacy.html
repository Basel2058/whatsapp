Privacy Policy

T.B.Technologies · Last updated 20 October 2026

T.B.Technologies uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to send its customers documents such as invoices, quotes and receipts, and to receive their replies. This policy explains what information that involves.

What we process

  • Your phone number and, if you set one, your WhatsApp profile name.
  • The documents and messages we send you.
  • The messages, files and photos you send to our WhatsApp number.
  • Delivery information from WhatsApp, such as whether a message was delivered.

Why

Only to send you the documents related to your business with us and to answer your messages. We do not use this information for advertising and do not sell it.

Who else sees it

Messages are carried by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms), which processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. Your replies are forwarded to our internal business email so our staff can answer them. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Our messaging service does not keep copies of messages or files after delivering them. Copies kept in our business email and accounting records are kept as long as needed for our business and as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop sending you documents by WhatsApp, or ask what information we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted, by sending a message to the WhatsApp number you received our messages from.
